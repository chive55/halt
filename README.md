# Halt

Privacy-first subscription tracker. No bank login.

Halt lives in the browser. Your list is stored on the device. It finds charges from a pasted statement, tracks trials, and opens real cancel pages when they exist. Gyms get a letter, not a fake one-click.

## Play now

https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/chive55/halt/blob/main/index.html

If styles fail to load in that preview, open the raw files locally or use:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/chive55/halt@main/index.html

## What v1 does

- Monthly total and unused / trial estimate
- Add, edit, cancel, pending
- Trial radar
- Statement paste parser
- Cancel guides for common services
- Gym cancellation letter download
- JSON backup / restore
- Installable PWA

## What v1 does not do

- Log into your bank
- Cancel a merchant for you
- Sync Apple or Google accounts
- Charge for a cancel pack

## Privacy

Data stays in `localStorage` on this device. There is no server.
